REQUIRED BEHAVIOR (plan section 4, in this order; each step is a test):
1. `select_history(store, *, workspace_id, access, selector, request_cutoff, clock)` (name yours, signature recorded in your evidence) resolves the selector with the injected UTC cutoff via `resolve_selector`; `compare` is rejected unless the caller supplies two independently pinned sides (implement single-side only; compare raises a clear `ValueError`).
2. Builds the broad retained proof with `EvidenceAccess` in `query_mode="history"`: live identity, workspace membership, artifact policy, complete AND support groups and `all_history` suppression apply BEFORE any time filtering. A `current_only` source tombstone (Task 1's) leaves authorized retained history visible.
3. Applies recorded and effective predicates (`match_temporal`) only to authorized `ObjectObservation` and `AssertionVersion` rows, emitting `recorded_match` as the reason for recorded-eligibility decisions (the deferred F7). Unknown-time rows go to a separate contextual inventory. Never adds an ID absent from the proof.
4. Computes the exact revision closure from selected observations and surviving complete support groups; includes only compatible retained link generations; records retention gaps without fabricating revisions or dates.
5. Persists a canonical `HistoryManifest` with sorted unique IDs, the exact resolved `selector_json`, the knowledge cutoff, and `coverage_json` holding separate proven/contextual counts and stable codes `history_unavailable`, `retention_gap`, `link_coverage_incomplete`; no private source text anywhere in it.
6. Builds `EvidenceSelection(revision_ids=..., assertion_version_ids=..., query_mode="history")`, acquires a `QuerySnapshot` that pins the manifest, and revalidates current authorization and suppression before each dispatch and on release. A cutoff before the earliest retained recorded interval returns `history_unavailable`, never current evidence.
7. Collection treats live and durable history snapshots as roots; a purge barrier overrides those roots and yields `evidence_purged` markers without retained text; `all_history` access loss or purge denies an old snapshot even when it was validly acquired.
8. `knowledge/model.py`: canonical sorted/unique validation for `HistoryManifest` and `ConflictSet` (reject unsorted or duplicate IDs, invalid support or interval shapes) while keeping unknown/open semantics; no opaque provider ordering or authority fields in JSON. `recorded_to` stays the only mutable historical field.
9. N1: `ResolvedTemporalSelector` rejects `known_at_source='latest'` when `known_at != latest_known_at` and `'inherited'` without a compare parent.
