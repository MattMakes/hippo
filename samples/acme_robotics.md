# The Acme Robotics Field Guide

## The company

Acme Robotics was founded in 2015 by Priya Natarajan. Acme Robotics is headquartered in Boulder. Acme Robotics builds robot arms for small factories. Priya Natarajan lives in Denver.

## Where things are

Boulder is located in Colorado. Denver is located in Colorado. The Boulder workshop is managed by Tomas Reyes. The Denver showroom is managed by Hana Sato.

## The Orion arm

The Orion arm is the flagship product of Acme Robotics. The Orion arm was designed by Marcus Lee. The Orion arm lifts 12 kilograms. The Orion arm was released in 2019.

## The Lyra gripper

The Lyra gripper is an accessory for the Orion arm. The Lyra gripper was designed by Hana Sato. The Lyra gripper is made of aluminium. The Lyra gripper was released in 2021.

## People

Marcus Lee studied at Colorado School of Mines. Marcus Lee reports to Priya Natarajan. Hana Sato studied at Kyoto University. Tomas Reyes reports to Marcus Lee.

## Software

The Orion arm runs Nimbus. Nimbus is written in Rust. Nimbus was created by Tomas Reyes. Nimbus depends on the Halo library.

## Customers

Blue Pine Bakery uses the Orion arm. Blue Pine Bakery is located in Fort Collins. Fort Collins is located in Colorado. Blue Pine Bakery is owned by Elena Voss.

## Suppliers

The Halo library is maintained by Skyline Software. Skyline Software is located in Portland. Portland is located in Oregon. Skyline Software was founded in 2010 by Omar Haddad.
